## Morning battle music

Out of combat: **“Fearless First” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**<br>
In combat: **“Sunshine” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**

Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License.<br>
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Sources: [Fearless First](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600058), [Sunshine](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100517).

“Fearless First” is slowed slightly to 88 BPM. The two tracks are edited into synchronized loops of approximately 98 seconds. “Fearless First” is used out of combat and “Sunshine” during combat.

Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License.<br>

## Afternoon battle music

Out of combat: **“Beachfront Celebration” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**<br>
In combat: **“Impact Allegretto” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**

Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License.<br>
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Sources: [Beachfront Celebration](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1200022), [Impact Allegretto](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100622).

Both tracks are edited into 80-second synchronized loops at 120 BPM. “Impact Allegretto” uses a section where the original track has already reached its climax, leaving out the introductory buildup. The game crossfades at the same playback position.

## Night battle music

Out of combat: **“Sunday Dub” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**<br>
In combat: **“Got Funk” — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**

Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License.<br>
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Sources: [Sunday Dub](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1700031), [Got Funk](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1200005).

“Sunday Dub” retains its 102 BPM tempo. “Got Funk” is adjusted slightly from 98 to 102 BPM. Both become 80-second synchronized loops. The combat track uses a clear funk arrangement of bass, drums, piano and brass, replacing a candidate with more distorted guitar.
